Feedback / Precision

These are precision / QA metrics — judgment consistency (human useful-rate) and evidence accuracy (automated judge accuracy). They are NOT validated sales lift: at MVP, feedback verdicts are the operator's own self-ratings, so a high useful-rate means the operator kept finding cards worth acting on, never that they closed deals. Every rate is shown with its sample size; an empty denominator reads as "not enough evidence yet", never 0%.

gate G2 — per-source demotion recommendations

Report-only (R9.5): < 40% precision over a source's rated cards is a recommendation to REVIEW (consider store-only / disable), never an auto-action — sources are never disabled automatically. A recommendation needs ≥ 20 rated cards; below that the sample is too noisy to act on.

R9.11 disagreement gate (KTD3): when a source's judge-vs-human disagreement exceeds 20% over enough comparable (audited ∩ human-rated) signals, its demotion recommendation is WITHHELD — the judge and humans disagree too much on that source to trust a demotion, so the rubric needs review before acting. Below the comparable floor the gate is "below floor" (too few to judge — never a fabricated block); with no comparable signals it is "n/a".
